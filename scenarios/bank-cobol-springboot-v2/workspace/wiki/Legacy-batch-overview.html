HPNIGHT nightly batch

Exported from Confluence (Mainframe Ops space). Author: P. Quinn. Last updated 2019-08-02.

The nightly job runs every calendar day. Steps: XFERPROC (apply the day's transactions), INTCALC (daily interest accrual), then on the last day of the month EODPOST (interest posting), FEEASSES (fees) and STMTGEN (statements).

Restart: rerun from the failed step with the previous generation of the master file.

Known quirks: see the program change logs. Ask Pat if in doubt.
